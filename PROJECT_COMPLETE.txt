✅ FEATURE 3: AUTO-SUBMISSION WHEN TIME EXPIRES
   Vietnamese: "thêm tính năng tự động nôp bài nếu phát hiện nút kết thúc"
   
   Implementation:
   - AutoSubmissionManager class
   - Background deadline monitoring
   - Automatic submit button click
   - Auto-submit status recording
   
   Process:
   1. Monitor time every 10 seconds
   2. Check: time_remaining <= 0?
   3. YES → Click submit button
   4. Record as "auto_submitted"
   5. Finalize quiz session
   
   Status: COMPLETE ✓
